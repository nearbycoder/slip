Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Slip finds nearby devices automatically and moves files directly between them.

Private by design

Files travel straight from one device to the other over HTTPS. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Automatic discovery

Devices on the same Wi-Fi show up on their own, with a subnet scan fallback for tricky networks.

Files, folders & text

Send anything: single files, whole folders, or a quick snippet of text or a link.

Accept or decline

Review every incoming transfer, or turn on Quick Save for devices you trust.

PIN protection

Require a PIN before anyone can send you anything, ideal on shared networks.

Fast

Parallel, streamed transfers at the full speed of your local network.

How it works

Three steps, no setup.

  1. Open Slip on both devices

    Make sure they're on the same network. They'll find each other in a second or two.

  2. Pick what to send

    Choose files, a folder, or type a message, then tap the device you want to send to.

  3. Accept on the other side

    The receiver gets a prompt, taps Accept, and the files land in their Downloads.

Works with LocalSend.

Slip implements the open LocalSend protocol v2, so it sends to and receives from any LocalSend app, as well as other Slip devices. Bring the people you share with along, whichever app they use.
